# Roadmap

## REL-001 — Design Freeze (In Progress)

Close remaining design-system implementation gaps, confirm tooling, reconcile live Notion/Figma references, and make MVP acceptance criteria executable.

## REL-002 — Internal Alpha v0.1 (Planned)

Build the Manifest V3 shell, local persistence, rules/protocol engine, guided-session core, timers, recovery, and the primary ten-screen path. Exercise it with controlled local data.

## REL-003 — Private Beta v0.2 (Planned)

Resolve research and usability findings, harden import/export and migrations, validate accessibility, add diagnostics and recovery coverage, and prepare contributor documentation.

## REL-004 — Public Launch v1.0 (Planned)

Complete security/privacy review, browser-store and open-source readiness, documentation, release checks, migration guarantees, and public launch materials.

No launch date is invented here. Scheduling depends on capacity and the current Notion plan.
